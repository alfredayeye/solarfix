Community Transformation Through Human Flight

Youth development, STEM education, social cohesion and skills pathways for Cape Town's historically disadvantaged communities. An initiative led by Ruben Knoetze, National and International Springbok Skydiving Champion.

Why this matters

The barrier is rarely ability. It is access.

Many young people in Cape Town's historically disadvantaged communities have limited access to structured recreational activity, new sporting disciplines, or environments that expose them to unfamiliar possibilities. South Africa has deep reserves of untapped talent and potential.

The Vertical Wind Tunnel introduces something genuinely new: an internationally recognised sport, in a safe and professionally supervised environment, that doubles as a platform for education, technology exposure, teamwork and personal development. The aim is not simply to teach people to fly. It is to broaden what participants believe is possible for themselves.

The programme

Five strands, one facility.

Access and youth integration

Young people from different socio-economic backgrounds participate, compete and build friendships side by side. Shared participation on equal terms is a direct route to breaking down divisions.

Personal development through sport

Indoor skydiving demands concentration, body awareness and discipline. Structured participation builds confidence, communication, teamwork, goal-setting, leadership and resilience.

STEM and education

A hands-on introduction to aerodynamics, airflow, engineering and physics, framed around an exciting practical experience rather than a classroom abstraction.

Skills development and career pathways

Instruction, coaching, technical operations, safety, events, media, hospitality and tourism. The programme exposes participants to these careers and, where possible, builds routes into training and employment.

Sporting pathway

From recreational flying through structured skills development to competitive indoor skydiving, coaching, and potentially national and international representation.

Disability inclusion

A dedicated programme for people with spinal cord injuries and paraplegia is a core part of this vision, addressed in full by the Cape Town School of Human Flight.

Community impact

What success looks like.

  • Greater access to structured, high-quality recreational activity
  • Meaningful interaction and relationship-building across communities
  • Youth development, confidence and personal growth
  • STEM and technology exposure for participants who might not otherwise get it
  • Skills awareness and career pathways into a new industry
  • A genuine sporting talent pipeline for South Africa

“A place where people do not simply learn to fly, but learn to believe in what is possible.”

Community and school partnerships

Reach depends on genuine partnerships.

The programme intends to work with schools, youth organisations, community groups, disability associations, government departments and corporate social investment programmes to identify participants and shape programmes around real community needs, ensuring access is not determined by a participant's ability to pay.

Partnership & sponsorship opportunities

Partners for the social development work.

  • Youth development programme sponsorship
  • STEM education initiatives
  • School and community outreach programmes
  • Bursaries and subsidised participation for disadvantaged communities
  • Skills development and career-pathway partnerships

Distinct from investment in the facility itself and from sponsorship of the disability programme. A detailed costing structure will be shared with prospective partners as part of due diligence.

Our vision

The dream of flying, made accessible.

Not as a privilege reserved for those with financial means, but as an experience open to young people, disadvantaged communities and people of all abilities. Success will be measured by opportunities created, barriers broken down, and the young people who leave the tunnel believing something new about what they are capable of.
